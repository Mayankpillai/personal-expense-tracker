🎓 Smart Student Spending Guidelines

1. The 50 / 30 / 20 Rule

Allocate 50% of your allowance to Needs (Food, Rent, Travel), 30% to Wants (Entertainment, Shopping), and 20% to emergency savings.

2. Daily Spending Cap

Divide your remaining balance by the remaining days in the month to find your daily maximum spending limit.

3. 24-Hour Purchase Rule

For any non-essential purchase over ₹500, wait 24 hours before buying to avoid impulse spending.
